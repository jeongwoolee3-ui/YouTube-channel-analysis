Design Concept and Visual Identity

Logo Meaning

The JJ logo was designed to create a modern and recognizable personal identity. The initials “JJ” form the main visual structure of the logo using clean and minimal typography. The star symbol placed above the initials represents creativity, ambition, innovation, and future growth.

The blue color reflects professionalism, reliability, and confidence, while the gold accent symbolizes creativity, success, and aspiration. The logo was designed to remain visually effective across multiple sizes and formats.
