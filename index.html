Main Analysis

Essay

01

Introduction

This website analyzes Mai Pham’s YouTube channel as a visual media text. The essay focuses on how the channel uses image, editing, personal branding, and lifestyle visuals to create meaning.

02

Visual Analysis

The channel presents daily life through bright visuals, personal camera angles, and casual editing. These choices make the videos feel close, emotional, and relatable to the audience.

03

Montage Analysis

The montage style connects different moments together and creates a smooth rhythm. It helps the viewer understand the creator’s mood, lifestyle, and identity without needing long explanations.

04

Conclusion

Through visuals, editing, and branding, the channel becomes more than simple entertainment. It creates a personal world that viewers can recognize, follow, and emotionally connect with.
